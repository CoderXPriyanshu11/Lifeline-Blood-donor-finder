About LifeLine

A SPPU SE AI&DS Community Engagement Project connecting blood donors with patients.

What is LifeLine?

LifeLine is a community-built web platform developed as part of the SPPU SE AI&DS Community Engagement Project (CEP). It connects voluntary blood donors with patients and families seeking blood donations across Pune and surrounding areas.

The platform provides a searchable, privacy-first directory of registered donors, a blood request submission form, community awareness campaigns, and an interactive survey to understand barriers to blood donation in the local community.

What LifeLine Does

  • Maintains a voluntary registry of blood donors with blood group, location, and availability.
  • Allows patients and families to find compatible donors in their city.
  • Accepts blood requests and shows matching donors.
  • Lists awareness and donation camps organised in partnership with blood banks.
  • Collects community survey data to understand donation barriers.
  • Gathers feedback to continuously improve the platform.

What LifeLine Does NOT Do

  • Does not perform medical screening of donors or recipients.
  • Does not determine blood compatibility — that is a clinical function.
  • Does not replace licensed blood banks or hospital transfusion services.
  • Does not administer, collect, store, or transport blood in any form.
  • Does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.
  • The "compatible blood groups" filter is a general reference only and must never be used as a substitute for professional compatibility testing.

Privacy Approach

  • Donors provide explicit, voluntary consent before registration.
  • Phone numbers and emails are masked publicly (e.g. 98XXXXXX10).
  • Full contact details are accessible only to the platform administrator.
  • Donors may request removal of their data at any time by contacting the admin.
  • All database queries use parameterised statements to prevent SQL injection.
  • Admin passwords are stored as secure Werkzeug hashes — never plain text.

Who Built This?

LifeLine was designed and built by SE AI&DS students at Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) as a Community Engagement Project under the guidance of faculty mentors and in consultation with local blood banks and the Indian Red Cross Society.

The project involved community surveys, stakeholder consultations, and awareness campaigns to ensure the platform addresses real needs in the community.

Disclaimer

LifeLine is a community connection platform only. All donation and transfusion decisions must be confirmed by authorised healthcare professionals and licensed blood banks. Always consult a qualified medical professional before donating blood or accepting a blood transfusion. Blood compatibility and donor eligibility assessments are purely clinical responsibilities and cannot be delegated to this platform.
