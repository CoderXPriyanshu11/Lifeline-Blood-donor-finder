Simple 3-Step Process

How LifeLine Works

01

Register as a Donor

Fill out a simple form with your blood group, location, and availability. Your consent is recorded and your contact details are protected.

02

Find or Request

Search available donors by blood group and city, or submit a blood request for a patient. Results show masked contact info to protect privacy.

03

Connect & Donate

Contact your local blood bank or hospital. A qualified healthcare professional handles all eligibility checks, compatibility testing, and the donation process.

🏥

Important Safety Notice

LifeLine Connects. Professionals Decide.

LifeLine is a community connection platform only. We do not perform medical screenings, eligibility assessments, or blood compatibility testing. All donation and transfusion decisions must be made by qualified healthcare professionals at a licensed blood bank or hospital. The compatibility suggestions on the search page are for general reference only and are not a medical determination.
